Skincare, Beyond the Hype.

Know what's in your skincare, and what the science actually says.

Skincare can be confusing. With so many products promising brighter, smoother, and healthier skin, how do we know which claims are actually supported by science?

Most of us care about our skin, but we don't have the time to research every ingredient like a scientist. Misleading claims can mean wasting money on products that don't work, or worse, using ingredients that may irritate or harm our skin.

That's why I built this AI research assistant. It helps you look up skincare products, explore their ingredients, and investigate what scientific research actually says about their effectiveness and potential risks.

How does it work?

The agent uses sources including Open Beauty Facts for product ingredient lists, PubChem for chemical information, PubMed and PubMed Central for scientific studies, and FDA cosmetic adverse event reports for reported safety concerns.

Not sure where to start?

Try asking whether an ingredient actually works, searching for a product by name, or checking if a moisturizer's advertised benefits are backed by research.

Research findings are informational and may be incomplete. Ingredient-level evidence doesn't necessarily prove that a finished product works, and adverse event reports do not establish causation.
